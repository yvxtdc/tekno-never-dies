About us

Who are we?

A sound system born in Lauterbourg, now an association that gets northern Alsace dancing.

It all starts with a shared wish: to bring electronic music to life close to home. A group of enthusiasts got the gear together, set up an association, then launched their own parties.

    The TND6TEM crew

    Members and volunteers run the technical, events, communication and creative teams.
