About us

An association of electronic music lovers based in Lauterbourg, in the Bas-Rhin.

Who are we?

  • MissionPromote, bring together, make it shake
  • GoalUnforgettable sound and visual experiences

Tekno Never Dies was born in Lauterbourg from a shared desire: to bring electronic music to life in Northern Alsace. A group of enthusiasts gradually became a collective, then an association.

We create parties where sound, lights and staging build a one-of-a-kind atmosphere. But a TND night is also about meeting people: familiar faces and newcomers coming together around the same energy.

The TND6TEM crew

Members and volunteers run the technical, events, communication and creative teams.

    Our story

    From an idea to a sound system.

    1. June 2023 The idea is born

      A shared passion for electronic music and the urge to organise events.

    2. 2024 The project takes shape

      Setting goals, building the crew, finding equipment and preparing the project.

    3. 4 December 2024 The association is founded

      Official registration of the association and launch of the first activities.

    4. June 2025 First achievements

      Organising the first events and developing technical services.

    What we do

    What drives us.

    • Events

      Designing and producing electronic parties, from the poster to the last bassline.

    • Sound system

    • Local projects

      Supporting initiatives and events run by other collectives in the region.

    • Volunteering

      Welcoming everyone who wants to get involved, and DJ set proposals.
