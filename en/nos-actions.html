The sound system TND6TEM

Speakers. Talent.

And sound that brings people together.

Our sound system is gear, a crew and DJs: everything it takes to turn an empty hall into a dancefloor.

Go backstage

01 Before the first note

Behind every bassline, hours of work.

Before the first note, there's an empty hall. We unload the gear, raise the truss, hang the lights and the booth, and wire up every speaker. Then we listen, adjust and start again, until the sound fills the whole space.

None of it happens alone: a sound system is a crew first.

Load. Build. Wire. Tune. Listen. Repeat.

03 The crew

One sound, one crew.

Setting up a sound system is teamwork: volunteers who carry, hang, wire and tune, night after night. That's what the association is about too.

  1. Organise

    We plan and run events, working on the line-up, tech, logistics and welcome.

  2. Amplify

    Our gear lets us build setups suited to all kinds of event formats.

  3. Support

    We can look into requests from associations, collectives or organisers looking for a technical solution.

  4. Pass it on

    The association is also about sharing knowledge and learning event tech together.
