Privacy

Information about data sent through the website.

Data controller

TEKNO NEVER DIES, an association registered under French law and based at 5 avenue Georges Clemenceau, 67630 Lauterbourg, is responsible for the data received through the form. Contact: tnd6tem@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

The form collects name, email address, type of request and message. The phone number is optional. Depending on the request, the person may also enter a date, an event, a place or a number of participants. This data is used solely to receive, understand and process the request, and to reply to it.

Legal basis

Processing is necessary to answer the request sent to the association and, where it concerns a collaboration project, to take the steps requested before a possible contractual relationship. Messages are not used to send advertising or newsletters.

Recipients and service providers

Submissions are accessible to authorised members of the association. The form is hosted by Formspree, which processes submissions in order to forward the messages. The site is published with GitHub Pages; GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged for security reasons. Formspree states that data may be processed in the United States or other countries. Before going live, the association must check its account's contractual terms, its data processing agreement and the safeguards applicable to transfers outside the European Economic Area.

Formspree privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy. GitHub privacy policy: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention period

Contact requests are kept for no more than three years after the last exchange with the person, then deleted from the mailbox and from Formspree. They are deleted sooner once they are no longer needed for follow-up. This period is a limit chosen for this processing, not a universal maximum set by law: the duration must remain justified by the purpose. Only items needed for a legal obligation or to defend a right may, if necessary, be set aside in restricted-access archives for the applicable period.

Reference: CNIL principles on retention periods (in French).

Your rights

Under the conditions set by the regulations, you can request access to, rectification, erasure or restriction of your data, object to certain processing and request data portability where this right applies. To exercise your rights, write to tnd6tem@gmail.com stating the subject of your request. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).

Audience measurement

The site uses GoatCounter, a cookie-free visitor statistics tool, to see which pages and which nights are viewed. For each page view, GoatCounter receives the page address, its title, the site you came from, the screen size and the browser type. The IP address is only used to estimate the country and to count a visit only once; it is not stored. Some clicks are also counted, with nothing that identifies you: ticketing, Instagram, directions, adding to a calendar, sharing, gallery photos and team profiles. The statistics are anonymous, cannot track a visitor from one site to another and are only used to improve the site. An ad or tracker blocker may disable this counting without affecting your use of the site.

GoatCounter privacy policy: goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Cookies and trackers

The site sets no cookies. It contains no advertising or embedded social content requiring a tracker, and the audience measurement described above works without cookies. The site's fonts and resources are hosted locally. Links to Instagram and other external sites only load their content after you click; their own rules then apply. Using the form may involve an exchange with Formspree when it is sent.

If you choose a language (French, German or English) in the menu, this choice is stored in your browser (local storage) for the following pages. It is not sent to the association or to any third party, and you can erase it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Changes to this policy

This page must be updated if the form, service providers, processing or site tools change. Last updated: 9 October 2026.
